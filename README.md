## Privacy & Owner-Only Wishes
- **Private Wishes System**: Guests can send wishes freely through the form.
- **Password-Protected Wishes Wall**: All received wishes and messages are kept private and hidden from general visitors.
- **Owner Unlock**: The couple/owner can click **"View Wishes (Owner Login)"** and enter the password to view all received wishes.
- **Accepted Passwords**: `2026RA`, `2026`, `ruban`, or `athulya`.
- **Lock Option**: The owner can click "Lock Wishes" at any time to hide them again.
